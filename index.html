About Me

Greetings! I am a Principal DevOps Engineer and AWS Certified Cloud Professional with 9 years of software engineering experience: 4 years in DevOps and 7 years as a mobile lead.

Based in Riyadh, Saudi Arabia on a transferable Iqama, I work with teams across Europe, the Middle East, and Asia, remotely or on-site. I am open to relocating to the job location.

My path into DevOps was sparked entirely by CI/CD automation. While serving as Lead iOS Engineer, I took ownership of the mobile release pipeline, building automated workflows with Fastlane and Xcode Cloud. That hands-on experience ignited a deep passion for infrastructure, automation, and scalable delivery systems. What started as improving app deployment grew into architecting full cloud environments on AWS. From January 2022, I transitioned into DevOps engineering while continuing to lead the mobile team in parallel, and I have been doing both ever since.

Today, as Principal Software Engineer at Salestech Data & AI GmbH, I wear two hats simultaneously. On the DevOps side, I design and manage cloud-native AWS infrastructure, Kubernetes (EKS) clusters with 2+ years of production experience, Terraform / Terragrunt IaC, GitOps with ArgoCD and FluxCD, and DevSecOps practices including SonarQube, Trivy, PCI DSS, and HIPAA compliance. On the mobile side, I lead the engineering team delivering enterprise iOS, Flutter, and React Native applications, with 30+ apps shipped and 4.5+ App Store ratings for clients across Europe, the Middle East, and Asia. That overlap is my edge: I architect the cloud infrastructure and ship the apps that run on it.
